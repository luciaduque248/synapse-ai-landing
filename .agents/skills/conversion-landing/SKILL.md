# Conversion Landing

Treat the landing page as one step in a funnel, not as a general company website.

## First establish
- Traffic intent/source when known (Meta Ads, Google Ads, organic, portfolio demo).
- One primary conversion event.
- One secondary action only when it supports the primary conversion.
- The promise the visitor must understand within the first viewport.

## Section rules
- Hero: state the offer, audience/value, and primary CTA without requiring scroll.
- CTA: use specific action labels. Avoid generic "Learn more" when a concrete action exists.
- Message match: keep the landing promise consistent with the campaign/ad intent.
- Proof: use relevant proof near points of uncertainty; do not invent fake customer logos, metrics, medical outcomes, or testimonials.
- Forms: ask only for fields needed for the next business step. Use labels, clear errors, autofill-friendly inputs, and explicit success states.
- Objections: answer the highest-friction questions before the final CTA.
- Mobile: keep the primary action reachable; a sticky CTA is allowed when it does not obstruct content or browser controls.

## Conversion quality
Do not use fake urgency, deceptive countdowns, pre-checked consent, misleading scarcity, or dark patterns. Portfolio demos may use clearly fictional sample data, but it must be labeled in project documentation.

## Review output
When reviewing a page, prioritize findings by expected conversion impact: blocker, high, medium, polish. Tie every recommendation to visitor intent, friction, clarity, trust, or actionability.

# Project Brief — SYNAPSE AI

## Purpose
This is a fictional portfolio case study built to demonstrate professional landing-page strategy, UX/UI, frontend implementation, responsive behavior, conversion thinking, interaction quality, SEO, and performance.

## Sector
B2B SaaS / AI productivity

## Primary business goal
Drive visitors to start a free trial or request a product demo.

## Conversion actions
- Primary: **Probar gratis**
- Secondary: **Ver demo**

## Visual direction
Sharp product-led SaaS: dark neutral foundation, controlled violet accent, crisp dashboard/product mockups, selective kinetic typography and stateful demos. Avoid generic glowing AI blobs, excessive gradients, and meaningless floating cards.

## Planned sections
- Product-led hero
- Interactive note-to-actions demo
- Outcome metrics placeholders
- Workflow before/after
- Feature bento with real interaction states
- Integrations
- Use cases
- Security/trust placeholders
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Trial CTA

## Portfolio integrity
- Brand, company, people, statistics, pricing, testimonials, credentials, availability, and results are fictional unless a future source explicitly makes them real.
- Do not invent social proof or claims that could be mistaken for verified facts.
- The final case study should clearly frame the work as a concept/portfolio project.
